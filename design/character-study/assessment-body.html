03 / Recommended sequence

Make the work clear.
Then make it lighter.

A bounded initiative keeps the work visible, the outcomes measurable, and exceptions with accountable people.

01

Days 1–30

Define the work.

  1. From the primary constraint

    Select one bounded workflow

    Choose a high-volume, rules-based process with clear pain, identifiable owners, and measurable outcomes.

  2. From the primary constraint

    Map the process and establish a baseline

    Document inputs, outputs, handoffs, delays, common exceptions, cycle time, effort, failure rate, and rework.

  3. From the next opportunity

    Select a repeatable process with clear rules

    Prioritize a process with meaningful volume, stable inputs, and a measurable operational outcome.
